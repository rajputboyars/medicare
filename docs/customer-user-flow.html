1

Create an account

Register at /register.

Name and mobile

Your name and a valid 10-digit mobile number are required. Order updates are sent to the mobile number.

Email and password

Email is optional. Password must be at least 8 characters and include a letter and a number.

Health-data consent

Consent is required to process prescriptions and orders for fulfilment and share what is needed with the pharmacy. You can withdraw this in Profile → Privacy.

You can browse and build a cart without an account, but signing in is required to place and track orders, save addresses, upload prescriptions, and use family/repeat-order features.

Already registered? Sign in

2

Search for a medicine

Start from Find Medicine or the home page’s nearby products.

Set your delivery area

Choose the location/pincode so the app can search pharmacies serving that area.

Search and filter

Search by medicine, generic name, brand, or health need. Filter by category, brand, prescription requirement, maximum price, and nearby availability.

Compare offers

Sort by fastest, cheapest, nearest, or a preferred pharmacy. Offers can show pharmacy, price, availability, and estimated time.

Add to your cart

Choose a pharmacy offer and add medicines to the cart. The cart is associated with a pharmacy; add items from the same pharmacy for one checkout.

Can’t find it? Use “Find this medicine for me” to ask nearby verified pharmacies. Replies can indicate availability, an alternative, out of stock, or an expected restock time.

3

Upload a prescription when needed

Prescription medicines cannot proceed to checkout without an attached prescription.

  1. Sign in and open Upload Prescription.
  2. Add a prescription photo or PDF; optionally select which family member it is for and enter medicine names to help find them.
  3. Give permission for the pharmacist to view the prescription, then upload it.
  4. Choose the uploaded prescription in the cart/checkout. The pharmacist reviews it before preparing.
  5. Check the prescription status and any review note on the upload page. If a clearer copy is requested, replace it from the order page.

Uploaded prescriptions are intended for the pharmacy fulfilling the order. The app states that delivery partners do not see prescription or medical details. The pharmacist may approve, reject with a reason, or ask for a clearer copy; medicines are not substituted automatically.

4

Check out and place your order

Sign in, provide delivery details, and review the quote before confirming.

Review medicines and prescription

Adjust quantities and remove items if needed. Attach a valid prescription for any Rx-required item.

Choose who the order is for

Select yourself or a family profile. Add family profiles in Profile → Family profiles.

Select an address

Choose a saved delivery address or add one with contact, house/area details, pincode, and a useful landmark.

Select delivery and payment

Choose normal or priority delivery if the pharmacy offers it. Select UPI, card, or cash on delivery. Wallet is not available yet.

Review price and estimate

Review item total, delivery fee, any applied offer, and the estimated delivery range. The estimate can change with stock, prescription review, and rider availability.

Place order and pay if needed

Place the order. For an unpaid online payment, open the order and use the payment action. The current local app describes this as a demo payment gateway.

5

Track and receive your delivery

Find orders under My Orders and open an order for detailed progress.

The order timeline can show order placed, prescription review/approval (when required), confirmed, pharmacy preparing, ready for pickup, rider assigned, picked up, out for delivery, and delivered.

  • While active, the order page shows an estimated time range and its factors.
  • After a rider is assigned, you can see rider details and call the delivery partner.
  • Show the delivery code on the order page to the rider only when you receive the parcel. Do not share it earlier.
  • For COD, pay the exact amount shown by the order/rider.
  • After delivery, view/print the invoice, rate the pharmacy, repeat the order, or save medicines for a refill reminder.

Cancellation and refunds: the interface says cancellation is free before rider pickup. For paid orders, refunds are shown as taking 3–5 working days to the original payment method. Available cancellation options depend on the order status.

6

Useful customer features

Manage repeat orders, family needs, and account preferences.

/repeat

Repeat & save medicines

Repeat a completed order or create a saved medicine list for yourself or a family member, with a reminder interval and optional preferred pharmacy. Prescription medicines still go through review; the app does not auto-buy them.

/family

Family profiles

Save a family member’s name, age, relationship, optional gender, blood group, allergies, current medicines, and preferred pharmacy for orders.

/profile

Profile & support

Manage addresses, English/Hindi language, large text, high contrast, consent, support tickets, and a downloadable copy of your account data.

i

Privacy and care reminders

Use the app for fulfilment support, not emergency medical advice.

  • Only share prescription/health information needed to fulfil your request, and review privacy consent in Profile.
  • Pharmacies preparing an order can review its prescription; delivery partners do not see medical item details.
  • Do not share the delivery code until the rider is handing over the order.
  • The app is not an emergency service. For urgent health concerns, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.
  • Availability, prices, delivery coverage, and estimated times depend on local pharmacy stock and service settings.
