V1 guide and build checkpoint

MediCare Local

Medicines delivered locally for small towns: customers order from nearby verified pharmacies, pharmacists check prescriptions, and delivery partners bring the parcel. Doctor, lab and home-care services are planned for V2.

For life-threatening emergencies, call 112 or 108. The app has an Emergency page, but it never promises an ambulance will arrive.

The guide

Who it helps, and how

Five roles use V1. Each gets a screen built for one job, so a first-time smartphone user can order medicine without training.

Customers and families

Find it, order it, track it

See which nearby pharmacies have a medicine, choose the fastest, cheapest or nearest, and follow the order to your door.

  • Search with stock shown per pharmacy
  • Family profiles: "who is this for?"
  • Landmark-friendly addresses
Elderly and non-technical users

Photo, number, done

Send a photo of the prescription with a mobile number and area. The pharmacy calls back. No account needed.

  • Upload Prescription & Call Me
  • Large text, high contrast, Hindi labels
  • Call and WhatsApp support in the header
Chronic patients

Never run out

Save diabetes, BP or thyroid medicines once, and repeat a previous order in one tap. Prescription medicines are still checked every time.

  • Previous orders with one-tap reorder
  • Saved medicines with refill dates
  • "Can't find your medicine?" asks nearby shops
Pharmacies

Online orders, pharmacist in control

Verify prescriptions, move each order from confirmed to ready, manage stock by CSV, and see payouts after commission.

  • Prescription verification queue
  • Inventory with low-stock alerts
  • Customers asking for a medicine you may have
Delivery partners

Accept, pick up, deliver

Go online, accept a delivery from the list, confirm pickup with a code, and finish with the customer's code. Medicine details stay hidden.

  • Available deliveries and one-tap accept
  • Navigate and call both sides
  • History and earnings
Platform admin

Launch town by town

Approve pharmacies and riders, switch pincodes on, generate settlements, handle complaints and watch the numbers.

  • Verification queue with reasons
  • Service areas and launch sign-ups
  • Audit log of sensitive actions

How an order moves

Eleven statuses, enforced in one place. Prescription orders add two review steps before the pharmacy confirms.

  1. Find itCheck your pincode, search a medicine, see stock at nearby pharmacies.
  2. PrescriptionUpload it when required. The pharmacist reviews and approves. Nothing is substituted.
  3. Pharmacy preparesConfirmed, preparing, then ready for pickup. Stock is reserved at confirmation.
  4. Rider picks upA rider accepts the delivery and collects it with the pharmacy's pickup code.
  5. Out for deliveryTrack the rider and call them. Cancel for free until pickup.
  6. DeliveredShare the 4-digit delivery code. Cash on delivery is collected. Reorder in one tap.
