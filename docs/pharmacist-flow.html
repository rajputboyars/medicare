1

Sign in and understand your access

Use the pharmacist account associated with your pharmacy at /login.

Successful pharmacist login routes to /pharmacy. The portal provides Dashboard, Orders, Prescription Verification, Medicine Requests, Inventory, Customers, Payments, Reports, and Settings navigation.

  • Pharmacist capabilities include reading/fulfilling pharmacy orders, reviewing prescriptions, and managing inventory.
  • Pharmacy management settings and pharmacy finance are owner-level permissions. Settings explicitly states only the pharmacy owner can change them.
  • The pharmacist role is linked to a pharmacy; server-side authorization applies to protected portal operations.

In a local development build, the login page offers a prefilled Pharmacist demo account. The demo password is displayed in the development-only login panel.

3

Review a prescription

Open Prescription Verification and select a prescription order in the review queue.

Open the uploaded document

Use View prescription to open the uploaded prescription. Review only for the pharmacy order you are handling.

Check key details

Check doctor name and registration, date, patient, and each medicine and strength against the order.

Approve a valid prescription

Select Approve prescription. The order can move to the new-order queue for confirmation.

Request clarification or reject

For an unclear copy, send a reason requesting a clearer copy. For an invalid prescription, reject with a reason shown to the customer.

Do not substitute medicines on your own. The portal instructs staff to request clarification if anything is unclear and to reject invalid prescriptions with a reason.

4

Handle “Call me” prescriptions

These are uploaded by customers who have not completed an in-app order.

  • View the prescription and call the customer using the callback number if present.
  • Confirm medicines and delivery details with the customer; invite them to place the order in the app.
  • When checked, mark it Verified, or select Cannot verify and provide a reason.

A call-me submission is a callback workflow, not an already-confirmed order. Confirm the order and price with the customer before fulfilment.

5

Process and hand off an order

Order cards show items, patient details relevant to the order, payment summary, prescription, and status.

Confirm the new order

For a non-prescription order or an approved Rx order, select Accept & confirm order.

Prepare

When ready to fulfil, select Start preparing.

Mark ready

After preparing and packing, select Mark ready for pickup.

Handoff to a rider

Assign the nearest rider or wait for an online rider to accept it. Give the rider the pickup code displayed on the order card.

Typical lifecycle: prescription review (if needed) → confirmed → preparing → ready for pickup → rider assigned → picked up → out for delivery → delivered. Actions are status-dependent; some transitions are performed by the rider or platform.

Reject or cancel

Eligible order cards provide Reject / cancel…, with a reason shown to the customer. Allowed actions depend on current order status. The ordinary pharmacy cancellation action is not available after the rider has picked up the order.

6

Maintain inventory

Inventory management is available to pharmacist and owner roles.

Add catalogue medicines with batch, expiry date, MRP, selling price, quantity, and low-stock threshold. Adjust quantity inline; use the CSV template for bulk upload.

  • CSV import supports up to 2,000 rows per upload and reports invalid rows by line.
  • Filter items by medicine name, brand, or generic name; show low stock only.
  • Inventory marks low-stock items and items expiring within 90 days.
  • When a nearby customer requests a medicine, report availability, an alternative, or out-of-stock/restock information. Add available items to inventory.
i

Privacy and role boundaries

Use prescription access only for legitimate review and fulfilment.

  • Prescription and health details are sensitive; the customer consents to share prescriptions with the pharmacy.
  • Only view prescriptions needed for your pharmacy’s order review or callback workflow.
  • Do not change or substitute prescribed medicines without appropriate authorization and customer/clinical process.
  • Riders do not receive medicine names or prescription contents.
  • Pharmacy owner-only areas include changing store settings and viewing pharmacy finance/settlements.
  • The app does not provide a pharmacist-specific credential/document upload or shift-management flow.
