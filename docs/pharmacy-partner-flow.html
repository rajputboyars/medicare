1

Apply to become a partner pharmacy

Open /partner/pharmacy or choose the pharmacy partner link from login.

Pharmacy name

Required; 3–100 characters.

Owner / pharmacist name

Required; enter the responsible person’s name.

Mobile and email

Both required; mobile and email must be valid.

Password

Required; 8–128 characters, including a letter and a number.

Drug licence number

Required; 6–60 characters.

GSTIN

Optional; if supplied, it must be a valid 15-character GSTIN.

Shop location

Required shop address, pincode, and district.

Service area and hours

Enter service pincodes (defaults to the shop pincode) and opening/closing times.

Required declaration: confirm licence details are correct, a registered pharmacist supervises dispensing, and the pharmacy will follow applicable pharmacy laws. Submission creates a pending application; duplicate mobile, email, or drug licence details are rejected. Registration is limited to three attempts per hour.

2

Licence review and sign-in

The platform team must verify the pharmacy before it can accept customer orders.

  • After submitting, the confirmation says the team will verify the drug licence.
  • You can log in at /login with the email or mobile and password used during registration.
  • While verification is pending, you can set up inventory; the pharmacy is not yet available to receive orders.
  • An administrator reviews pharmacies in Admin → Verification queue → Pharmacies. The queue reminds reviewers to check the drug licence against state drug-control records and confirm a registered pharmacist.
  • Verification decisions are audit logged. Once verified, the pharmacy owner can enable order acceptance in Settings.

Development demo: In non-production builds, login provides demo account buttons including Pharmacy owner and Pharmacist. The displayed development password is for local demo access; it is separate from a new partner application.

4

Review prescription orders

Prescription medicines require pharmacist review before the order can proceed to preparation.

Open the review queue

From the dashboard or Prescription Verification page, open a prescription order and its uploaded prescription.

Check the prescription

Check the doctor’s name and registration, date, patient, and every medicine and strength against the order.

Approve when valid

Select Approve prescription. The order can then move into the new-order queue for confirmation.

Ask for a clearer copy or reject

If unclear, ask the customer for a clearer copy with a reason. If invalid, reject with a reason. The reason is shown to the customer.

Important: The portal explicitly instructs pharmacy staff not to change or substitute medicines on their own. If any detail is unclear, request clarification.

“Call me” prescriptions

For a call-me prescription, view the prescription and call the customer if a callback number is available. Confirm the medicines and delivery details, invite them to place the order in the app, then mark it verified or reject it with a reason. Call-me requests are not ordinary confirmed delivery orders.

5

Process an order and hand it to delivery

Order cards show the order status, patient/order details, items, payment summary, and available next action.

Accept and confirm

For a new non-prescription order, or after the prescription is approved, select Accept & confirm order.

Start preparing

When the order is confirmed and the pharmacy is ready to fulfil it, select Start preparing.

Mark ready for pickup

When the parcel is prepared, select Mark ready for pickup. The order becomes eligible for rider assignment.

Arrange rider pickup

Assign the nearest rider or wait for an online rider to accept the offer. The pharmacy order card displays the pickup code to give the rider at handoff.

Order status path: New / prescription review → confirmed → preparing → ready for pickup → rider assigned → picked up → out for delivery → delivered. Some states are advanced by the customer, pharmacist, rider, or platform rather than by the pharmacy portal.

Reject or cancel

Eligible orders have a Reject / cancel… action that requires a reason. The allowed action and order-state rules depend on the current status. After a rider has picked up an order, pharmacy staff cannot use the ordinary pre-pickup cancellation path.

6

Keep inventory accurate

Inventory data feeds availability for customers and can prevent overselling.

Add or update a catalogue medicine with its batch, expiry date, MRP, selling price, quantity, and low-stock alert threshold. Use the CSV template and upload for bulk entry; CSV imports support up to 2,000 rows per upload, and invalid rows are reported with row numbers.

  • Filter by medicine name, brand, or generic name; filter to low-stock items.
  • Update a quantity directly from the inventory list and save the change.
  • The app flags low stock and items approaching expiry (within 90 days).
  • When customers request an unavailable medicine, respond “We have it,” suggest an alternative, or mark it out of stock. Add available medicines to inventory so customers can order them.
7

Payments, settlements, and reports

Use the portal to review transaction and operating summaries.

Payments & settlements

Review paid amounts, COD due for rider collection, online payments awaiting payment, refunds, and settlement rows with period, order count, gross, commission, net payout, and status.

Reports

Review order totals, delivered and cancelled counts, cancellation rate, prescription-order share, revenue and order charts for the last seven days, top medicines, and repeat-order share.

Settlement boundary: the platform creates settlements for delivered orders and the portal shows their status. It does not provide a pharmacy-side manual payout request or payment processing control in this flow.

8

Configure store settings

Only the pharmacy owner can change these settings.

  • Set opening and closing hours.
  • Set service pincodes and a delivery radius from 1 to 30 km.
  • Choose whether to offer priority delivery.
  • Turn Accepting orders on or off after the pharmacy is verified; it cannot be enabled while verification is pending.
i

Operational and privacy boundaries

Current implementation details to keep in mind.

  • Pharmacy accounts only manage their own pharmacy’s orders, stock, and settings.
  • Prescription content is available to pharmacy users involved in review and fulfilment; handle it as sensitive health information.
  • Customer records show masked phone numbers in the customer list.
  • The rider receives parcel count and delivery/handoff information, not medicine names or prescription contents.
  • Self-registration captures licence number and a declaration, but does not include a licence-document upload or automated licence verification. A platform administrator performs the implemented review.
  • GSTIN is optional. The form does not request bank account details or a pharmacist document upload.
