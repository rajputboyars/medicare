1

Sign in as platform admin

Admin access is restricted to the SUPER_ADMIN role.

Use the authorized administrator account at /login. A successful SUPER_ADMIN login routes to /admin. Admin API handlers enforce read/write permissions; do not share administrator credentials.

In non-production local builds, the login page has a Platform admin demo button and displays the demo password. Do not use development demo credentials in production.

2

Use the platform dashboard

The dashboard refreshes its live summary periodically and links to operational queues.

Demand & commerce

Orders and GMV

Orders today, live orders, GMV excluding cancelled orders, customer count, deliveries completed, and cancellations.

Partner operations

Pharmacies & riders

Active pharmacies, pending pharmacy approvals, riders online/verified, and pending rider approvals.

Exceptions

Requests, complaints, settlements

Open medicine requests, open support tickets, and settlements awaiting processing or payment.

3

Verify pharmacy and rider partners

Open Verification queue and select the partner type.

Review submitted details

Pharmacy records include licence, owner, address, service pincodes, and contact details. Rider records include name, mobile, and vehicle information.

Check the pharmacy licence

The UI directs reviewers to compare the drug licence against state drug-control records and confirm a registered pharmacist.

Verify or reject

Verify an applicant to activate the partner. Reject a pending application with a reason sent to the applicant.

Audit the decision

Verification changes are audit logged. The service supports pending, verified, rejected, and suspended statuses.

Access effect: pending/unverified partners cannot receive orders (pharmacy) or go online and accept deliveries (rider). Rejected/suspended partners are disabled by the verification service.

Manage pharmacy and rider records

The Pharmacies page shows verification, licence, pincode, orders, revenue, inventory lines, and rating; administrators can suspend a verified pharmacy with a reason or reactivate a suspended one. The Riders page shows contact, vehicle, verification, online status, delivery count, and earnings; administrators can suspend or reactivate riders.

4

Monitor marketplace operations

Use the portal navigation to inspect the platform-wide workload.

Orders

Newest-first order list with order code/status, customer, pharmacy, rider, total, payment status, Rx indicator, and creation time.

Customers

Account-level list with contact, join date, order count, spending, and family-profile count. The admin customer table explicitly excludes prescriptions and health details.

Medicine requests

See requested medicine, pincode, request state, pharmacy reply count, available replies, and timestamp.

Service areas

Add state/district/town/pincode coverage entries as inactive, then activate/deactivate them. Customers outside coverage see “We’re coming to your area” and can leave their mobile number.

Complaints

Review support requests with subject, message, customer, related order, status, and timestamp. Move open tickets to in progress, then resolve them.

Payments

Inspect online and COD payments, refunds, provider, amount, status, reference, and creation time.

5

Generate and process settlements

Settlement actions are managed from the Settlements page.

  1. Select Generate settlements to create pending settlement records for partner delivered orders not yet settled.
  2. Review partner/type, period, order count, gross, commission, net payout, and status.
  3. Move pending settlements to Processing with “Start payout.”
  4. After the payout is completed externally, mark the settlement Paid.

The portal records settlement status; mark Paid only after the actual payout is completed. The described page does not itself initiate a bank transfer.

6

Configure platform pricing

Platform Settings changes apply to new orders only.

Settings expose platform commission percentage, normal and priority delivery fees, order threshold for free normal delivery, normal and priority rider payouts, and support phone number. Review changes carefully before saving because they affect new order quotes and payout calculations.

7

Review reports and audit history

Use reports for trends and the audit log for accountability.

Reports

Seven-day orders and GMV; top pharmacies and medicines; cancellation reasons; prescription share; total, found, and open medicine requests.

Audit log

Newest-first record of the last 300 events, including time, actor role, action, target type, and target ID. Verification decisions and other audited changes can be reviewed here.

i

Administrative guardrails

Keep access, personal information, and financial actions controlled.

  • Admin read/write operations require SUPER_ADMIN authorization; use named accounts and protect credentials.
  • Use verification evidence and document reasons for rejection/suspension where required.
  • Customer admin listings omit prescriptions and health details; do not seek or expose health data without an authorized operational need.
  • Use customer payment and settlement records to reconcile status; do not treat a UI status change as proof a transfer occurred.
  • Service-area changes affect whether customers can order in an area.
  • Changes to commission, customer delivery fees, rider payouts, and support contact affect new orders/platform operations.
