1

Apply to become a rider

Submit the delivery-partner form at /partner/rider.

Your name

Required; 2–80 characters.

Mobile number

Required; must pass mobile-number validation.

Email

Required; enter a valid email address.

Password

Required; 8–128 characters, with at least one letter and one number.

Vehicle

Required; enter the vehicle type and details. Example: Motorcycle UP34 AB 1234.

After Apply: The app creates a rider profile and login, marked Pending and Offline. Duplicate mobile numbers or emails are rejected. The registration endpoint is limited to three attempts per hour.

2

Wait for verification, then sign in

A platform administrator reviews new rider applications.

In the admin area, reviewers open Verification queue → Riders and can verify an applicant or reject a pending application with a reason. Decisions are audit logged.

  • Sign in at /login using the email or mobile number and password entered on the application.
  • Unverified riders see an account notice and cannot go online or receive available offers.
  • After approval, sign in and switch availability to Online to see eligible deliveries.

Development demo: In non-production builds, expand “Demo accounts (development only),” choose Rider, and use the demo password shown on that page. The seeded demo rider is already verified; a new application remains pending until reviewed.

3

Explore the rider app

The signed-in rider experience has three main sections.

/delivery

Deliveries

Online/offline switch, today’s earnings and count, active jobs, and available offers.

/delivery/history

History

Completed delivery reference, area, completion time, payout, and cash collected.

/delivery/earnings

Earnings

Daily, seven-day, and all-time summaries, a seven-day chart, and COD cash-in-hand reminder.

The rider app can be installed as a separate web app on supported devices. Installing it does not enable offline delivery actions; delivery data and actions require an internet connection.

4

Review and accept an offer

Only verified riders who are online can see unassigned, ready-for-pickup jobs.

An offer may show the order code, ready time, parcel count, payout, pharmacy and distance, customer drop area and distance, priority status, and COD collection amount. Offers are ordered by distance when available, then ready time.

Before accepting: the exact customer address, name, and contact details are hidden. After you select Accept delivery, the job is assigned if it is still available. If another rider took it first, the app reports that it is no longer available.

5

Complete a delivery

Follow the order of steps shown on the active delivery card.

Go to the pharmacy

Use the call or directions links to reach the pickup location.

Confirm parcel pickup

Ask the pharmacy for its 4-digit pickup code. Enter it and select Confirm pickup. Before pickup, you can release a job you cannot complete.

Start delivery

After pickup, select Start delivery. The customer is notified that the order is on its way and to have their delivery code ready.

Navigate to the customer

After accepting, the app displays the customer’s name, phone, address, and landmark. Use call or map directions when available.

Collect COD cash if shown

For cash-on-delivery orders, collect the exact amount displayed in the app.

Confirm delivery

Ask the customer for the 4-digit delivery code. Enter it and select Complete delivery to finish the job.

On successful completion, the order is marked delivered, the payout and completion are recorded, and the customer is notified. For COD, payment is marked paid as part of delivery completion.

6

Review your history and earnings

Use the bottom navigation to check completed work.

Delivery history

Shows up to 100 completed deliveries with order code, area and pincode, delivery date and time, payout, and cash collected where applicable.

Earnings

Shows today’s amount and delivery count, last-seven-days earnings, all-time earnings, a daily chart, and today’s COD cash-in-hand reminder when applicable.

Payouts: the platform team settles payouts. The app shows earning summaries but does not offer an in-app withdrawal or payout request flow.

7

Privacy and important boundaries

Delivery tools are intentionally limited to information needed for handoff.

  • Medicine names, prescription contents, and medical details are not shown to riders. Keep parcels sealed and handle them carefully.
  • Customer identity and exact contact/address details are revealed only after a job is accepted.
  • A rider can update only jobs assigned to that rider.
  • A rider can release an assigned job only before pickup; the rider flow does not offer job release after pickup.
  • COD cash is recorded when the delivery is completed; the app does not transfer or reconcile physical cash.
  • Pickup and delivery codes are four digits. Order-action requests are limited to eight per rider/order in a ten-minute window.
i

What is not part of the current onboarding

This guide reflects the features implemented in the app today.

  • Identity document or driving-licence upload.
  • Profile photo or bank/payment details.
  • Preferred rider service-area form.
  • Automated identity or licence checks.
  • Rider-side application status tracking.
  • In-app payout withdrawal/request flow.
